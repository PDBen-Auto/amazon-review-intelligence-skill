What it does well

Evidence-preserving collection, repeatable Excel delivery, offline review analysis, need-based VOC themes, switching triggers, and product improvement hypotheses.

What it does not claim

It does not promise every historical review, infer population defect rates, bypass Amazon controls, or replace managed proxy infrastructure and formal customer research.
