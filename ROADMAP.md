# Roadmap

## Near term

- Add more synthetic marketplace fixtures without storing customer or account data.
- Improve evidence coverage summaries for partial pages and translated text.
- Expand offline report examples for product, packaging, and compatibility decisions.

## Later

- Add adapter contracts for additional public review sources where lawful access is available.
- Add reproducible benchmark fixtures for deduplication and theme attribution quality.

## Non-goals

- CAPTCHA or account-control bypasses.
- Hidden telemetry, prompt injection, or covert tracking.
- Replacing legal, platform-policy, or customer-data review.
